Atal Tunnel Travel Guide

Published by Travel With Jhechaa • June 2026

Introduction

Atal Tunnel is one of India's greatest engineering achievements and a major attraction near Manali.

About Atal Tunnel

  • Length: 9.02 km
  • Altitude: 10,000+ feet
  • Connects Manali with Lahaul Valley
  • Open throughout the year

What Makes It Special?

The tunnel significantly reduces travel time between Manali and Lahaul while offering year-round connectivity.

Nearby Attractions

  • Sissu Waterfall
  • Sissu Lake
  • Lahaul Valley
  • Snow View Points

Best Time To Visit

The tunnel can be visited throughout the year. Winter visits often include snow-covered landscapes.

Travel Tips

  • Start early in the morning.
  • Carry warm clothing.
  • Check road conditions during heavy snowfall.
  • Visit Sissu after crossing the tunnel.

Conclusion

A trip through Atal Tunnel is one of the highlights of any Manali vacation and should not be missed.
