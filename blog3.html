The Problem

Many travel agencies and local shops add extra commissions on adventure activities, cab bookings, and sightseeing tours. Drivers and agents also work on commission-based systems, which increases the final cost for tourists.

As a result, travelers sometimes pay high prices but still do not get the best experience or service quality.
