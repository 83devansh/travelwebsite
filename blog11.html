Best Cafes In Manali

Published by Travel With Jhechaa • June 2026

Introduction

Manali is famous not only for mountains and adventure but also for its vibrant cafe culture. Whether you want great coffee, delicious food or breathtaking views, these cafes are worth visiting.

Top Cafes In Manali

  • Cafe 1947
  • Drifters Cafe
  • Johnson's Cafe
  • Rocky's Cafe
  • Sunshine Cafe
  • The Lazy Dog
  • Born Free Cafe

Best Cafes For Views

Many cafes in Old Manali offer beautiful river and mountain views, making them perfect for relaxing after sightseeing.

Average Cost

  • Coffee: ₹150 – ₹350
  • Meals: ₹300 – ₹800
  • Desserts: ₹150 – ₹400

Conclusion

Exploring Manali's cafes is an experience in itself. From local Himachali dishes to international cuisines, there is something for everyone.
