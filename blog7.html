How To Reach Manali

Published by Travel With Jhechaa • June 2026

Introduction

Manali is one of India's most popular hill stations and can be reached through multiple transportation options. Here's a complete guide to help you plan your journey.

By Bus

Volvo buses are the most popular way to reach Manali.

  • Delhi to Manali: 12–14 hours
  • Chandigarh to Manali: 8–10 hours
  • Shimla to Manali: 7–9 hours

Many overnight Volvo buses operate daily.

By Car

Self-driving to Manali is an unforgettable experience.

  • Delhi to Manali: ~540 km
  • Chandigarh to Manali: ~300 km
  • Shimla to Manali: ~250 km

The journey offers beautiful mountain views, rivers and tunnels.

By Flight

The nearest airport is Bhuntar Airport (Kullu-Manali Airport).

  • Distance from Manali: 50 km
  • Taxi available from airport

By Train

Manali does not have its own railway station. The nearest railway stations are:

  • Joginder Nagar Railway Station
  • Chandigarh Railway Station
  • Kalka Railway Station

Best Option For Most Travelers

For convenience and affordability, overnight Volvo buses from Delhi remain the most popular choice.

Travel Tips

  • Book Volvo tickets in advance during peak season.
  • Carry motion sickness medicine if required.
  • Start early if driving yourself.
  • Check weather and road conditions before departure.

Conclusion

Whether you travel by bus, car, train or flight, reaching Manali is part of the adventure. Choose the option that suits your budget and schedule.
