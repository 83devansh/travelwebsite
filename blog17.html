Hampta Pass Trek Guide

Published by Travel With Jhechaa • June 2026

Introduction

Hampta Pass Trek is one of the most famous treks in Himachal Pradesh. It offers an incredible transition from the lush green Kullu Valley to the barren landscapes of Lahaul.

Trek Highlights

  • Altitude: 14,100 feet
  • Duration: 4–5 Days
  • Difficulty: Moderate
  • Suitable for Beginners
  • Amazing Campsites

Best Time To Visit

  • June
  • July
  • August
  • September

During these months, the trail remains open and weather conditions are generally favorable.

Trek Route

  • Manali
  • Jobra
  • Chika
  • Balu Ka Ghera
  • Hampta Pass
  • Chatru

What To Pack?

  • Trekking Shoes
  • Warm Jackets
  • Rain Protection
  • Water Bottle
  • Power Bank
  • First Aid Kit

Fitness Requirements

Basic fitness is recommended. You should be comfortable walking for 5–7 hours per day.

Conclusion

Hampta Pass Trek is perfect for adventure lovers seeking dramatic scenery, mountain landscapes and unforgettable camping experiences.
