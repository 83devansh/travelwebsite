History Of Manali

Published May 2026 · 6 min read

Manali is a famous Himalayan town located in Himachal Pradesh. The name “Manali” comes from Sage Manu, who is believed to have recreated human life here after a great flood.

Origin Of The Name

The word “Manali” is derived from “Manu-Alaya” which means “The Home of Manu.”

Ancient Culture

The region has deep connections with Hindu mythology, temples, and local Himachali traditions.

British Era

During British rule, Manali became popular because of its cool climate and scenic beauty.

Modern Tourism

Today, Manali is one of India’s top travel destinations, attracting adventure lovers, bikers, trekkers, honeymoon couples, and tourists from all over the world.
