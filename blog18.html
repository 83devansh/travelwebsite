Bhrigu Lake Trek Guide

Published by Travel With Jhechaa • June 2026

Introduction

Bhrigu Lake is one of the most beautiful alpine lakes near Manali and is famous for its breathtaking views and mythological significance.

Trek Overview

  • Altitude: 14,100 feet
  • Duration: 3–4 Days
  • Difficulty: Easy to Moderate
  • Starting Point: Gulaba

Why Is Bhrigu Lake Famous?

According to local legends, Sage Bhrigu meditated near the lake, making it an important spiritual destination.

Best Time To Visit

  • May
  • June
  • July
  • August
  • September

Major Attractions

  • Alpine Meadows
  • Snow-Capped Peaks
  • Camping Experience
  • Mountain Photography
  • Beautiful Sunrise Views

What To Pack?

  • Trekking Shoes
  • Warm Clothing
  • Sunscreen
  • Water Bottle
  • Trekking Pole
  • Rain Jacket

Travel Tips

  • Acclimatize properly.
  • Stay hydrated.
  • Start the trek early.
  • Carry necessary medicines.

Conclusion

Bhrigu Lake Trek combines adventure, spirituality and natural beauty, making it one of the best trekking experiences near Manali.
