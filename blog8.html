Manali Snowfall Guide 2026

Published by Travel With Jhechaa • June 2026

When Does Snowfall Start In Manali?

Snowfall generally begins during late December and continues until February. January is usually the peak snowfall month.

Best Places To Experience Snowfall

  • Solang Valley
  • Sethan Valley
  • Sissu
  • Atal Tunnel North Portal
  • Rohtang Pass Area

Winter Activities

  • Skiing
  • Snowboarding
  • Snowmobile Rides
  • Snow Photography
  • Snow Trekking

Things To Pack

  • Thermals
  • Waterproof Jacket
  • Snow Boots
  • Gloves
  • Woollen Cap

Travel Tips

Always check weather updates and road conditions before traveling during winter. Book hotels in advance during Christmas and New Year holidays.

Conclusion

For snow lovers, January is the best month to visit Manali. Fresh snowfall, winter sports and breathtaking landscapes create unforgettable memories.
