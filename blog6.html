Top 15 Places To Visit In Manali

Published by Travel With Jhechaa • June 2026

Introduction

Manali is one of India's most beautiful mountain destinations. Whether you're seeking adventure, spirituality, photography, or relaxation, Manali has something for everyone. Here are the 15 best places you should visit during your Manali trip.

1. Hadimba Devi Temple

A famous wooden temple surrounded by cedar forests and one of Manali's most visited attractions.

2. Solang Valley

Known for paragliding, ATV rides, skiing and breathtaking mountain views.

3. Atal Tunnel

One of the world's longest high-altitude tunnels connecting Manali with Lahaul Valley.

4. Sissu

A beautiful village famous for waterfalls, mountains and peaceful scenery.

5. Rohtang Pass

A legendary mountain pass offering snow and spectacular Himalayan landscapes.

6. Jogini Waterfall

A short trek leading to one of the most beautiful waterfalls near Manali.

7. Old Manali

Popular for cafes, nightlife, local culture and backpacker vibes.

8. Mall Road

The shopping and food hub of Manali.

9. Vashisht Temple & Hot Springs

Known for natural hot water springs and spiritual significance.

10. Sethan Valley

A hidden paradise famous for snowfall and camping.

11. Bhrigu Lake

One of Himachal's most scenic alpine lakes.

12. Hampta Pass

A world-famous trekking destination.

13. Naggar Castle

Historic architecture and stunning valley views.

14. Nehru Kund

A natural spring located on the Manali-Leh highway.

15. Burua Village

Experience authentic Himachali culture away from tourist crowds.

Conclusion

These destinations showcase the true beauty of Manali. From ancient temples to thrilling adventures and hidden villages, each place offers a unique experience.
