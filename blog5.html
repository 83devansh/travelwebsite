Complete Manali Trip Cost Guide 2026

Published by Travel With Jhechaa • June 2026

Introduction

One of the most common questions travelers ask is: "How much does a trip to Manali cost?" The answer depends on your travel style. Whether you're a student looking for a budget trip or a couple planning a luxury vacation, this guide will help you estimate your expenses accurately.

Transportation Cost

Delhi To Manali Volvo Bus

  • Standard Volvo: ₹900 – ₹1,500
  • Luxury Volvo: ₹1,500 – ₹2,500

Private Taxi

  • Sedan: ₹8,000 – ₹12,000
  • SUV: ₹12,000 – ₹18,000

Self Drive

Fuel cost from Delhi to Manali is approximately ₹4,000 – ₹7,000 depending on vehicle type.

Accommodation Cost

Budget Hotels & Hostels

  • Hostel Dorm: ₹500 – ₹1,000 per night
  • Budget Hotel: ₹1,000 – ₹2,000 per night

Mid-Range Hotels

  • ₹2,500 – ₹5,000 per night

Luxury Resorts

  • ₹6,000 – ₹20,000+ per night

Food Expenses

Food costs vary significantly depending on where you eat.

  • Budget Traveler: ₹300 – ₹600/day
  • Mid Range Traveler: ₹800 – ₹1,500/day
  • Luxury Traveler: ₹2,000+/day

Adventure Activity Costs

  • Paragliding: ₹2,500 – ₹4,500
  • River Rafting: ₹800 – ₹1,500
  • ATV Ride: ₹500 – ₹2,000
  • Zipline: ₹500 – ₹1,200
  • Zorbing: ₹300 – ₹800
  • Skiing: ₹1,000 – ₹3,000

Local Sightseeing Cost

Local sightseeing usually includes:

  • Hadimba Temple
  • Mall Road
  • Old Manali
  • Vashisht Temple
  • Jogini Waterfall
  • Solang Valley

Taxi cost for local sightseeing: ₹2,000 – ₹5,000 per day

Sample Budget For A 3-Day Trip

Budget Traveler

  • Transport: ₹2,000
  • Hotel: ₹2,000
  • Food: ₹1,500
  • Sightseeing: ₹1,500

Total: ₹7,000 – ₹10,000

Mid Range Traveler

  • Transport: ₹3,000
  • Hotel: ₹9,000
  • Food: ₹3,000
  • Sightseeing: ₹3,000

Total: ₹15,000 – ₹22,000

Luxury Traveler

  • Transport: ₹10,000+
  • Hotel: ₹20,000+
  • Food: ₹8,000+
  • Activities: ₹10,000+

Total: ₹40,000+

Money Saving Tips

  • Travel during off-season.
  • Book hotels early.
  • Use shared cabs when possible.
  • Choose package deals.
  • Book adventure activities through trusted local operators.
