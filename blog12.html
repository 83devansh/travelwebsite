Solang Valley Activities & Prices

Published by Travel With Jhechaa • June 2026

Why Visit Solang Valley?

Located around 14 km from Manali, Solang Valley is the adventure capital of Himachal Pradesh.

Popular Activities

  • Paragliding
  • ATV Riding
  • Zipline
  • Zorbing
  • Snow Scooter
  • Skiing
  • Horse Riding

Approximate Prices

  • Paragliding: ₹2,500 – ₹4,500
  • ATV Ride: ₹500 – ₹2,000
  • Zipline: ₹500 – ₹1,200
  • Zorbing: ₹300 – ₹800
  • Skiing: ₹1,000 – ₹3,000

Best Time To Visit

Summer for adventure activities. Winter for skiing and snow experiences.

Travel Tips

  • Wear comfortable clothes.
  • Carry sunglasses.
  • Book activities through trusted operators.
  • Arrive early to avoid crowds.

Conclusion

Solang Valley offers unforgettable adventure experiences for all age groups and remains a must-visit destination in Manali.
