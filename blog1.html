Top Places To Visit In Manali

Published May 2026 · 5 min read

Manali is one of the most beautiful hill stations in India, famous for snow mountains, adventure sports, rivers, forests, and peaceful valleys.

1. Solang Valley

Solang Valley is famous for paragliding, skiing, snow activities, and ATV rides. It becomes a snow paradise during winters.

2. Rohtang Pass

Rohtang Pass offers breathtaking mountain views, glaciers, and snow-covered roads.

3. Hadimba Temple

A historic wooden temple surrounded by cedar forests, known for its peaceful atmosphere and unique architecture.

4. Old Manali

Old Manali is popular for cafes, music culture, local markets, and relaxed vibes.

5. Jogini Waterfall

A beautiful waterfall reached through a scenic trek filled with nature and mountain views.
