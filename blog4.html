Best Time To Visit Manali In 2026

Published by Travel With Jhechaa • June 2026

Introduction

Manali is one of India's most beautiful hill stations located in Himachal Pradesh. Surrounded by snow-capped mountains, pine forests, rivers, and adventure activities, it attracts travelers throughout the year. However, every season offers a different experience. Some visitors come for snowfall while others prefer pleasant summer weather. In this guide, we'll help you choose the best time to visit Manali in 2026 based on your travel goals.

Summer Season (March To June)

Summer is considered the most popular time to visit Manali. The weather remains pleasant and comfortable for sightseeing.

  • Temperature: 10°C to 25°C
  • Perfect for family vacations
  • Ideal for sightseeing
  • Best time for paragliding
  • Excellent weather for river rafting
  • Clear mountain views

Popular places like Solang Valley, Hadimba Temple, Mall Road, Atal Tunnel and Sissu remain easily accessible during summer.

Monsoon Season (July To September)

Monsoon transforms Manali into a lush green paradise. Waterfalls become more beautiful and valleys look fresh.

  • Temperature: 12°C to 22°C
  • Less tourist crowd
  • Cheaper hotel prices
  • Beautiful greenery
  • Photography opportunities

However, travelers should be aware of possible landslides and occasional road blockages during heavy rainfall.

Winter Season (October To February)

Winter is the favorite season for snow lovers. During December and January, Manali often receives snowfall and becomes a winter wonderland.

  • Temperature: -2°C to 10°C
  • Snowfall in nearby areas
  • Ideal for honeymoon couples
  • Skiing opportunities
  • Snow adventures

The best places to enjoy snow include Solang Valley, Sethan Valley, Sissu and the North Portal of Atal Tunnel.

Best Month For Snowfall

If your main goal is to experience snowfall, plan your trip between late December and early February. Fresh snowfall is most common during this period.

Best Month For Families

Families generally prefer April, May and June because the weather remains pleasant and road conditions are excellent.

Best Month For Adventure Activities

Adventure lovers should visit between March and June. Activities available include:

  • Paragliding
  • River Rafting
  • ATV Riding
  • Zipline
  • Trekking
  • Camping

Travel Tips For 2026

  • Book hotels early during summer holidays.
  • Carry warm clothes even during summer evenings.
  • Check weather forecasts before winter travel.
  • Pre-book adventure activities during peak season.
  • Travel with local guides for hidden destinations.

Conclusion

The best time to visit Manali depends on your travel preferences. For pleasant weather and sightseeing, visit between March and June. For snowfall and winter adventures, December and January are ideal. No matter which season you choose, Manali offers unforgettable experiences, breathtaking scenery, and exciting adventures.
