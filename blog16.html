Sethan Valley Travel Guide

Published by Travel With Jhechaa • June 2026

Introduction

Sethan Valley is one of the most beautiful hidden destinations near Manali and has become famous for snowfall and winter camping.

Why Visit Sethan Valley?

  • Heavy Snowfall
  • Camping Experience
  • Igloo Stays
  • Stargazing
  • Peaceful Environment

Distance From Manali

Sethan Valley is located around 15 km from Manali.

Best Time To Visit

  • December to February for snow
  • March to June for pleasant weather

Popular Activities

  • Snow Trekking
  • Skiing
  • Camping
  • Photography

Conclusion

Sethan Valley offers a peaceful alternative to crowded tourist destinations and is perfect for nature lovers.
