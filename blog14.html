Sissu Travel Guide

Published by Travel With Jhechaa • June 2026

Introduction

Located in the beautiful Lahaul Valley, Sissu has become one of the most popular destinations near Manali after the opening of Atal Tunnel.

Top Attractions

  • Sissu Waterfall
  • Sissu Lake
  • Lahaul Valley Views
  • Mountain Photography Spots

Distance From Manali

Sissu is approximately 40 km from Manali and can be reached within 1 hour through Atal Tunnel.

Best Time To Visit

  • March to June
  • September to November
  • Winter for Snow Lovers

Things To Do

  • Photography
  • Nature Walks
  • Snow Activities
  • Camping

Conclusion

Sissu is a perfect destination for travelers looking for peaceful landscapes, waterfalls and Himalayan beauty.
