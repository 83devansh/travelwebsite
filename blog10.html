Hidden Places In Manali

Published by Travel With Jhechaa • June 2026

Why Explore Hidden Places?

While most tourists visit popular attractions, Manali has many peaceful locations that remain unexplored.

1. Sethan Valley

A beautiful Buddhist village known for snowfall, camping and stargazing.

2. Burua Village

Experience authentic Himachali culture and stunning mountain views.

3. Lamadugh Trek

A less crowded trek offering spectacular views of the Pir Panjal range.

4. Jana Waterfall

A peaceful waterfall surrounded by forests and traditional Himachali restaurants.

5. Hamta Village

A scenic village perfect for photography and nature lovers.

Travel Tips

  • Travel with local guides.
  • Carry cash for remote villages.
  • Respect local traditions.
  • Avoid littering.

Conclusion

Hidden destinations offer a more authentic experience and allow travelers to discover the true beauty of Manali beyond crowded tourist spots.
