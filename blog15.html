Rohtang Pass Travel Guide

Published by Travel With Jhechaa • June 2026

About Rohtang Pass

Rohtang Pass is one of Himachal Pradesh's most iconic mountain destinations located at an altitude of around 13,050 feet.

Why Visit?

  • Snow Activities
  • Mountain Views
  • Photography
  • Adventure Trips

Permit Information

Visitors may require permits depending on government regulations and seasonal restrictions.

Best Time To Visit

  • May to October
  • Winter access may be restricted

Travel Tips

  • Carry warm clothes.
  • Start early in the morning.
  • Carry valid ID proof.
  • Check permit availability before travel.

Conclusion

Rohtang Pass offers unforgettable Himalayan scenery and remains one of the top attractions near Manali.
